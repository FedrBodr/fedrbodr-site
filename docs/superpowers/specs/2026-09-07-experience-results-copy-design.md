# Experience results copy design

## Goal

Strengthen the homepage experience section by replacing a years-first narrative
with evidence of scale, shipped work, and current product results. Keep the
existing visual structure and update Russian and English copy together.

## Message hierarchy

1. Lead with the range of responsibility: nationwide banking transaction flows
   through current AI-assisted products.
2. Support the claim with concrete, attributable work: Sberbank reconciliation,
   Crypterium payment integrations, and a team of up to 13 people.
3. Connect past experience to current outcomes: Vezdepost in production with
   30+ platforms, AhMiranami generating revenue, KSY Store in a referral pilot,
   and the Marcel Lior site delivered to production.
4. Close on the core promise: taking products from idea to a working result.

## Copy changes

- Replace the section headline in both languages.
- Replace the lead paragraph in both languages with the approved results-led
  narrative.
- Replace the current five credential cards with:
  - 15+ years of commercial development;
  - a team of up to 13 people;
  - banking, crypto-wallet, and payment-system experience;
  - 30+ platforms supported by Vezdepost;
  - products in production and revenue-generating projects.
- Keep the existing company list and LinkedIn link as supporting proof.

## Fact boundaries

- Use only claims already present in the repository or supported by public
  professional materials.
- Describe Sberbank work as processing a nationwide daily transaction flow, not
  as sole ownership of the system.
- Describe KSY Store as being in a referral pilot and AhMiranami as generating
  revenue; do not invent customer, revenue, or usage numbers.
- Do not use token counts or test counts in the homepage section because they
  describe individual development sessions rather than customer value.
